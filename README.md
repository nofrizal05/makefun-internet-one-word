One word for the whole internet — funniest and most fitting wins.

**The word: Bottomless.**

A bottomless pit of tabs, threads, videos and rabbit holes. You can
scroll forever and never reach the end of it. It makes you laugh,
then nod — because it is true.

## Entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only) writes the single word
to `/output/entry.txt` (honouring `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`) and prints it.
